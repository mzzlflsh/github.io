Meelde — free Estonian language learning app

Learn Estonian words, phrases and grammar with flashcards — in your browser or as an app on your phone. No sign-up, no ads, works offline.

Open Meelde

Free · Levels A1–B1 · English and Russian interface

What’s inside Meelde

  • Word and phrase flashcardsEstonian words, phrases and sentences with translations and examples. Swipe right for “I know”, left for “I don’t know”.
  • Spaced repetitionCards you don’t know come back more often, cards you’ve learned less often. Every session is a fresh mix.
  • Vocabulary by topic and levelOver 5,000 cards at levels A1–B1: food, home, city, work, health, shopping and more. Turn off the topics you don’t need.
  • Estonian grammar guideRules with tables and examples: cases, verbs, pronouns and more. Jump from a card straight to the related rule.
  • Daily goal and statisticsSet a daily goal and see how many cards you’ve learned and which ones are still hard.
  • Works offlineAfter the first visit Meelde works without the internet. Your progress stays on your device.

Who it’s for

  • beginners learning Estonian from scratch;
  • people living in Estonia or moving here who want to manage everyday situations — shops, doctors, work;
  • learners who studied Estonian before and want to grow their vocabulary and review grammar;
  • self-learners who want a little practice every day.

Interface in English or Russian, card translations in English or Russian.

How to start

  1. Open meelde.ee in your phone or computer browser.
  2. Tap “Start session” and answer the cards: “I know” or “I don’t know”.
  3. To install it on your phone: on iPhone — Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android — Chrome → ⋮ menu → Install app.

Questions and answers

Is Meelde really free?

Yes. It’s a non-commercial project: no subscription, no payments, no ads and no sign-up.

Is Meelde good for beginners?

Yes. New users start with basic A1 words. A2 and B1 topics, phrases and sentences can be switched on in the Dictionary section.

Can I study without the internet?

Yes. You only need a connection the first time you open it. After that the app works offline, and the dictionary updates whenever you’re back online.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your device — nothing is sent to a server. To move your progress to another phone or browser, save a backup in Settings.

Meelde (Estonian for “into memory”, as in meelde jätma — to remember) is a free, non-commercial project for learning the Estonian language (eesti keele õppimine).
